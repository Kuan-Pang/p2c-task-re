# Hand-built M0 example tasks

Three tasks written by hand for M0 (PLAN §11): one F2 forward, one F4 backward, one F5. They exist to
exercise the harness — `grade.py`, the in-process runner, the oracle/nop sanity runs and the V5-style
separation checks — not to be benchmark items. Both source papers are in the **`train`** half of the
frozen split and are two of the five pilot papers, so prompts and principles get tuned on them; they are
excluded from eval under any later split. Provenance is `handbuilt`, and the gates (V1–V5, D) have not
been run on them.

Each folder's hand-written files are `instruction.md` and `tests/{question.md, rubric.json, gold.json}`.
`tests/question.md` is exactly what the solver is sent, `instruction.md` without its canary line and the
blank line after it; the packager refuses a folder where the two disagree.
Everything else is generated by the packager (docs/task_format.md §1, §8) — `task.toml`,
`environment/README.md`, `solution/solve.sh` (from `gold.json`), `tests/test.sh`, and verbatim copies of
`tests/grade.py` and `tests/judge_prompt.md` — so edit the hand-written files, never the generated ones,
and re-sync from the repo root:

```bash
uv run python -m p2c_task_re.stages.package --sync examples/tasks --split train --provenance handbuilt
```

`tests/test_package.py` fails whenever a generated file differs from what this command would write.
Answer-envelope fixtures for each task live in `tests/fixtures/ablations/<task_id>/`, with an
`expected.json` giving the per-criterion verdict a careful judge should return, the consistency verdict
and the expected pass/fail.

Seed ids (`t06`, `t12`, …) are row indices into the paper's `*.extraction.json` `table_2`; the question
ids (`N02`, `N06`, `N07`) are from the verified pilot mix.

## `2026.02.17.706358-f2-fwd-01` — F2, forward

**Paper.** Api5–FGF2 in MCF10A breast epithelial 3D acinar culture (pilot paper P1, seed `N02`).
**Seed triplet.** Gold `t08`, the cross-pathway readout (Tier C null, `single_experiment`), paired with
the two non-null arms `t06` (Akt-branch inhibition) and `t07` (MEK/ERK-branch inhibition); premises also
drawn from `t02` and `t05`. A Tier C null never carries a gold alone (PLAN §8), hence the pairing.

**Withheld target.** The four phospho readouts in singly treated Api5 OE spheroids: pERK1/2 after
Akt-branch inhibition and pAkt T308 after UO126 (both **unchanged**), plus the two same-branch
engagement readouts (both **decreased**). The two cross-pathway arms and one on-target arm are
`critical`, so every constant-enum policy scores `s_core = 0`.

**What makes it derivable.** The premise set gives the phenotype dissociation: Akt-branch inhibition
reverts volume, surface area, cell number, sphericity and Ki-67 but leaves filled lumens and all three
polarity markers untouched, whereas MEK/ERK inhibition reverts those *and* restores hollow lumens,
Laminin V, GM130 and E-cadherin. A branch that fed the other would have carried the other's phenotypes
with it. The second licensing premise is that neither inhibitor changed Api5 or FGF2 levels, so the
shared FGF2/FGFR1/FRS2α input is intact in both arms.

**Decision points.** DP1 — parallel branches versus one branch acting through the other (the wrong fork
is the textbook PI3K/Akt–Ras/ERK crosstalk, which predicts a fall in the untouched branch). DP2 —
unchanged versus a *rise* in pAkt T308 after MEK inhibition through relief of ERK-dependent negative
feedback; the unchanged upstream drive excludes it.

**Gold quote (Results, "Ras/MAPK/ERK signalling regulates Api5-FGF2-mediated proliferation, lumen
filling and polarity disruption during later stages of morphogenesis").**
> To check if there was crosstalk between Akt and ERK signalling pathways, pERK1&2 activation upon Akti
> addition and pAkt T308 levels upon UO126 addition were investigated (Figure S4j-m). No observable
> changes were detected, thereby indicating the absence of any crosstalk between the two signalling
> cascades.

The on-target arms are reported in the same Results section ("A decrease in pERK1&2 activation was
observed upon UO126 addition in both day 12 and 16 Api5 OE spheroids compared to untreated") and in the
PDK1/Akt section. Neither the title nor the abstract mentions crosstalk or any inhibitor arm.

## `2026.04.25.720790-f4-bwd-01` — F4, backward

**Paper.** PRMT7 restricts CD8⁺ T-cell expansion (pilot paper P3, seed `N06`).
**Seed triplet.** Gold `t12` (SGC3027); distractors `t10` (Prmt7 cKO) and `t18` (MS54 PROTAC), the
paper's other two perturbations of the same target on the same start state, each with its own reported
CD8⁺ outcome. Premises from `t13`, `t15` and `t17`.

**Withheld target.** Which of the three perturbations produced the revealed outcome — the CD8⁺ fraction
at about 11% against about 30% in its matched control after 72 h of αCD3/CD28 activation. Gold:
`sgc3027`. The other two arms are reported to *raise* the CD8⁺ fraction (cKO ~30% → ~48%; MS54 ~15% →
~30%), so exactly one option fits.

**What makes it derivable.** Two premises. (i) Wild-type PRMT7 and an enzymatically inactive mutant
co-immunoprecipitate p65 equally and repress p65-driven NF-κB reporter activity to the same degree, so
the restraint on p65 survives loss of catalysis. (ii) MS54 removes PRMT7 protein through the
ubiquitin–proteasome system and, in T cells, reproduces the deletion's rise in nuclear p65, while
SGC3027 leaves the protein in place yet demonstrably occupies it in cells (pre-treatment abolishes
MS54-induced degradation). With the NF-κB profile of PRMT7-deficient T cells (targets and survival genes
up, apoptosis programmes down, difference abolished by BAY 11-7082), both protein-removal arms must
raise the CD8⁺ fraction, so the falling arm is the catalytic inhibitor.

**Decision points.** DP1 — modality: the PROTAC groups with the genetic deletion, not with the catalytic
inhibitor whose warhead it is built from (the wrong fork groups the two small molecules). DP2 —
catalysis: blocking methyltransferase activity does not relieve the brake (the wrong fork treats a
catalytic inhibitor as a chemical knockout, which flips the answer to the degrader). DP3 — direction:
NF-κB de-repression expands rather than depletes the CD8⁺ compartment.

**Gold quote (Results, "Prmt7 deficiency increases CD8+ T effector cell cytokine production and
cytolytic activity").**
> Unlike CD8+ cells from Prmt7 cKO, which showed an increase from ∼30% to ∼48% (Figures 2G, 2H, bars
> with red dots), SGC3027 treatment did not promote CD8⁺ expansion. Instead, a drastic decrease was
> observed in SGC3027-treated CD8+ T cells, dropping from ∼30% to ∼11% (Figures 2G, 2H, bars with blue
> dots), accompanied by a modest increase in CD4⁺ T cells.

Neither SGC3027 nor any catalytic-inhibitor arm appears in the title or the abstract.

## `2026.04.25.720790-f5-01` — F5, direction-neutral

**Paper.** Same PRMT7 paper (pilot paper P3, seed `N07`).
**Seed triplet.** Gold `t15` (PRMT7WT versus catalytically dead PRMT7 on p65); premises from `t10`,
`t12`, `t18` and `t16`. This task *reveals* the SGC3027 and MS54 CD8⁺ outcomes that the F4 task above
withholds, and withholds the catalytically-dead-mutant result that the F4 task reveals — the two tasks
are graded independently and are never shown to the same solver in one trial.

**Withheld target.** The paper's mechanism for the split between the arms: PRMT7 restrains NF-κB
**non-catalytically**, the protein itself holding p65/RelA in the cytoplasm, so the brake scales with
PRMT7 protein level and is lifted only by removing the protein. The three `core: true` criteria grade
exactly that; the RelA-binding and cytoplasmic-retention facts are revealed premises, because the
abstract states them.

**What makes it derivable.** The three arms differ in one variable — whether PRMT7 protein is still
there — and the arm that leaves it there is shown to engage PRMT7 in cells, which removes the
"inhibitor never worked" escape. Together with PRMT7 being cytoplasmic, binding p65 and raising
cytoplasmic p65 when overexpressed, that points to a stoichiometric binding function rather than an
enzymatic one.

**Decision points.** DP1 — real catalysis-independence versus a technical failure of the inhibitor arm
(settled by the competition experiment). DP2 — protein abundance versus catalytic output as the variable
that sets nuclear p65 (the wrong fork is the prior-friendly "PRMT7 methylates p65 or a partner").

**Gold quote (Results, "Prmt7-deficiency leads to CD8+ T cell transcriptional reprogramming").**
> Increasing amounts of expression vectors encoding GFP-PRMT7WT or GFP-PRMT7DEAD into sgCTL and sgPRMT7
> B16.F10 melanoma cells repressed the p65-mediated luciferase activity, in a dose-dependent manner,
> suggesting that Prmt7 represses NF-κB activity in a methyltransferase-independent manner (Figure 3I).

The abstract's mechanism sentence — "Mechanistically, PRMT7 associated with RelA and restricted its
nuclear translocation" — is revealed in the premises and, naming a methyltransferase, points the
unprepared reader towards a catalytic mechanism rather than away from one.

## Point arithmetic

| task | `w_core` | judge points | gold `s` | correct core, no rationale | correct core + wrong mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `…-f2-fwd-01` | 0.60 | 10 over 5 criteria (2 of them 0-point notice items) | `s_core` 1.0, `s_judge` 1.0, **s = 1.00** | `s_judge` 0.0 → `s` 0.60, fails | `s_judge` 0.0 → `s` 0.60, fails |
| `…-f4-bwd-01` | 0.60 | 13 over 7 criteria (3 notice items) | `s_core` 1.0, `s_judge` 1.0, **s = 1.00** | `s_judge` 0.0 → `s` 0.60, fails | `s_judge` 0.0 → `s` 0.60, fails |
| `…-f5-01` | 0.40 | 11 over 5 non-core criteria (2 notice items) + 3 `core` criteria | `s_core` 1.0, `s_judge` 1.0, **s = 1.00** | `s_judge` 0.0 → `s` 0.40, fails | `contradiction` → `s` 0.00, fails |

For F5 the core *is* the mechanism, so "correct core + wrong mechanism" contradicts itself: its rationale
argues a catalytic mechanism against its own non-catalytic `mechanism_primary`. The consistency check
fires and zeroes every judged criterion, the three `core` ones included, so `s_core` is 0 as well.

Each task's `tests/fixtures/ablations/<task_id>/expected.json` carries the same arithmetic for every
ablation.
